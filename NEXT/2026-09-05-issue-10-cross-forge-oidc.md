## Add the cross-forge OIDC coordinator contract

Source: `2026-09-03-issue-10-cross-forge-oidc-coordinator.md` (preserved in `docs/changelog-migration/2026-09-05/`).


Authenticate forge workloads with complete exact-claim OIDC policies and trusted HTTPS JWKS mappings. Derive recoverable request identities from stable client nonces, persist both dispatch intents before delivery, reclaim crashed workers through fenced leases with stable idempotency keys, and accept independent signed receipts through create-once completion.

## Add cross-forge OIDC entrypoints

Source: `2026-09-03-issue-10-cross-forge-oidc-entrypoints.md` (preserved in `docs/changelog-migration/2026-09-05/`).


Exchange native GitHub and GitLab identities through protected, immutable coordinator origins with redirect rejection and bounded requests. Compile GitLab's origin into its protected component, submit stable pipeline nonces for crash recovery, then poll the accepted request identity for matching signed evidence.
